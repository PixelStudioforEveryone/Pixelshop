Pixelshop rendering references — 2026-10-12

The mask composition model and rendering stage dependencies were adapted to
HarmonyOS ArkTS/OpenGL ES with reference to lightcraft. Compositor informed
the brush/compositing review. Pixelshop's bounded bilateral denoiser is its
own implementation; it is not lightcraft's guided-filter/AI denoiser or
Compositor's Camera Raw implementation. No upstream photos, icons, fonts,
camera matrices, measured colour tables or model weights are included.

lightcraft: https://github.com/storytold/lightcraft
